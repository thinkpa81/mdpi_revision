# JTAER revised submission figures

This archive contains 7 main-text and 4 supplementary figures, each provided as a 600 dpi PNG, vector PDF, vector SVG, and source CSV. The plotting files are copied without modification from the analytical outputs; manuscript numbers and final captions follow the revised documents.

- `Main/`: Figures 1–7.
- `Supplementary/`: Figures S1–S4.
- `data/`: corresponding aggregate plot data (and the workflow diagram text for Figure 1).
- `FIGURE_CROSSWALK.csv`: publication numbers, analytical source filenames, and full final captions.
- `FIGURE_CAPTIONS.md`: captions for editorial use.
- `FIGURE_MANIFEST.json`: dimensions, resolution, source-document hashes, and per-figure hashes.
- `SHA256SUMS.json`: SHA-256 checksums of every other file in this archive.

Figure 7 is the 84-cell comparison of block contributions in development, whole temporal, and person-disjoint evaluation. Development entries are descriptive; uncertainty qualifications are specified in its caption. Figure 4 uses 1,000 complete-procedure bootstrap repetitions per focal setting. No respondent-level data are included.
