The Korean core question is identical in all three instruments: 귀하는 생성형 AI를 이용한 경험이 있습니까? A close English translation is Have you ever used generative AI? Response1 is 이용한 경험이 있다. and response2 is 이용한 경험이 없다. No explicit recall period is attached to this question.

The preceding definition pages are PDF16/20/28 (printed15/19/27). The definition and principal modalities remain substantively the same. From2023 to2024, the text changes ChatGPT to its Korean transliteration and replaces Bard with Gemini; the accompanying Google screenshot is also replaced. The2025 definition retains the2024 examples and images, but changes the Part D heading and omits the introductory instruction about changes to daily life. Follow-up questions about the type of service change, but these are separate items asked after the binary experience question.

The published codebooks confirm codes1 and2 throughout. Their Variable Lables sheets record q23/Q24/q20; the detailed codebooks label the question numbers Q23/Q24/Q20. Stable core wording and response coding are evidence against a coding reversal, but do not establish measurement invariance. Updated context, sample composition and real behavior could all contribute to changes in reported experience.

The2025 A item is included in this instrument audit for completeness; only2023 A and2024 A enter the two original prediction transitions.

All six PDF pages were visually inspected. File hashes and precise workbook row locators are in a_item_wording_audit.json.
